How to use

  1. Allow camera permission when prompted.
  2. Choose a camera from the dropdown (if you have multiple).
  3. Point device to a QR / barcode. Result appears instantly.
  4. Or click Upload and choose an image file.

Features

  • Camera scan with camera selection.
  • Image upload fallback.
  • Toggle torch (if device supports it).
  • Pause / resume scanning, copy/open results.
